Fortuity Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2025

Protecting your confidential information is our priority. This Privacy Policy applies to Fortuity (“Fortuityco.com”, “Fortuity”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) and governs data collection and usage for our website, products, and services. By using Fortuityco.com, you consent to the data practices described in this statement.

Collection of Your Personal Information

To better provide you with products and services, Fortuity may collect personally identifiable information, such as:

  • First and Last Name
  • Mailing Address
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Employer
  • Job Title
  • Application Credentials

If you purchase Fortuity’s products and services, we collect billing and credit card information to complete the transaction. We may also collect anonymous demographic information, such as age, gender, race, and household income.

We do not collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide it. You may be required to provide certain personal information when you use specific products or services, such as registering for an account, entering a contest, signing up for offers, sending us an email, or submitting payment information.

Use of Your Personal Information

Fortuity collects and uses your personal information to operate and deliver the services you have requested. We may also use your information to inform you of other products or services available from Fortuity and its affiliates.

Sharing Information with Third Parties

Fortuity does not sell, rent, or lease its customer lists to third parties. We may share data with trusted partners to help perform statistical analysis, send you email or postal mail, provide customer support, or arrange for deliveries. All such third parties are prohibited from using your personal information except to provide these services and are required to maintain confidentiality.

We may disclose your personal information if required by law or in good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal process, protect and defend the rights or property of Fortuity, or protect the personal safety of users or the public.

Opt-Out of Disclosure of Personal Information

You have the right under applicable privacy laws (including the California Consumer Privacy Act, CCPA) to opt-out of the disclosure of your personal information. If you exercise this right, we will refrain from disclosing your information unless you subsequently provide express authorization.

To opt-out, please contact us at:
Email: privacy@fortuityco.com

Security of Your Personal Information

Fortuity secures your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure using industry-standard security measures. While we strive to protect your information, no data transmission over the Internet or any wireless network can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Right to Deletion

Upon receipt of a verifiable request, we will delete your personal information from our records and direct service providers to do the same, subject to certain exceptions (such as completing transactions, complying with legal obligations, or detecting security incidents).

Children Under Thirteen

Fortuity does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of thirteen. If you are under thirteen, you must ask your parent or guardian for permission to use this website.

Opt-Out & Unsubscribe from Communications

You may opt out of receiving marketing or promotional communications from Fortuity by following the unsubscribe instructions in those communications or by contacting us at privacy@fortuityco.com.

External Data Storage Sites

We may store your data on servers provided by third-party hosting vendors with whom we have contracted.

Changes to This Statement

Fortuity reserves the right to change this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you about significant changes by placing a prominent notice on our website and/or updating any privacy information. Your continued use of Fortuityco.com after such modifications constitutes your acknowledgment and agreement to the updated policy.

SMS Terms of Service

By providing your mobile phone number to Fortuity, you consent to receive SMS messages from us related to your account, transactions, and promotional offers. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

  • You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying “STOP” to any message you receive from us.
  • For help, reply “HELP” or contact us at privacy@fortuityco.com.
  • We do not share your mobile number with third parties for their marketing purposes.
  • Delivery of SMS messages is subject to effective transmission by your mobile service provider and may not be available in all areas.
